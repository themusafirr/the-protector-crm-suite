### Page 2 — Health Insurance Checklist
```
🏥 HEALTH INSURANCE — MUST-HAVES

□ Family Floater (not individual) — cheaper
□ Cover ≥ ₹5 Lakh (city tier-2 hospital costs)
□ Cashless 10,000+ hospitals (check network)
□ Pre-existing cover after 2-4 years wait
□ No-claim bonus 5-50% (renew yearly)
□ Day 1 cover — no waiting for accidents
□ Tax save u/s 80D — ₹25k/year

💡 TIP: Family Floater 4 members = ₹25k/year
   Individual 4 plans = ₹60k/year (DOUBLE)
```

### Page 3 — Bike Insurance Checklist
```
🏍️ BIKE INSURANCE — MUST-HAVES

□ Third-party cover (mandatory by law)
□ Own damage (zero-dep add-on) — ₹500 extra
□ Personal accident ₹1 Lakh (PA cover)
□ Engine protection (new bikes, geo-extended)
□ NCB protection (no-claim bonus retained)
□ Cashless garage network (near you)
□ Online claim process (30-min approval)

💡 TIP: 5 saal claim-free = 50% discount on premium
   ₹800×5 = ₹4,000 saved on renewal
```

### Page 4 — Car Insurance Checklist
```
🚗 CAR INSURANCE — MUST-HAVES

□ Comprehensive (not just third-party)
□ Zero-dep add-on (full claim without depreciation)
□ Engine protection (especially new cars)
□ No-claim bonus protection (NCB shield)
□ Roadside assistance 24x7
□ Consumables cover (parts replaced)
□ Personal accident ₹15 Lakh (owner-driver)
□ Key replacement + lockout cover

💡 TIP: Renewal se 7 din pehle compare karo
   Average savings = ₹6,500 between 4 companies
```

### Page 5 — Life Insurance Checklist
```
👨‍👩‍👧 LIFE INSURANCE — MUST-HAVES

□ Term plan (not endowment/savings)
□ Cover = 10× annual income (minimum)
□ ₹1 Cr cover — pure protection, no return
□ Claim ratio > 97% (IRDAI rating check)
□ Premium ≤ 8% of monthly income
□ Tax save u/s 80C — ₹1.5L + 80C(10) ₹50k
□ 30-year policy lock (long-term safety)
□ Riders: critical illness, accidental death

💡 TIP: LIC se 2x sasta — Max Life / HDFC Life
   Same ₹1 Cr cover, half the premium
```
