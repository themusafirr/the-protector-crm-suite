THE PROTECTOR Health Insurance Checklist

🏥 Health Insurance

Family Floater (not individual) — saves up to 50% premium
Minimum cover ₹5 Lakh (city tier-2 hospital cost)
Cashless network ≥ 10,000 hospitals pan-India
Pre-existing cover after 2-4 years waiting
No-claim bonus 5% → 50% (5 saal claim-free)
Day 1 cover — no waiting for accidents
Tax save u/s 80D — ₹25,000/year
Restore benefit (cover reload if exhausted)
Free annual health checkup
AYUSH treatment cover included

Individual plans for same family = ₹60,000/year (2.4x costly)

THE PROTECTOR Bike Insurance Checklist

🏍️ Bike Insurance

Third-party cover (mandatory by law)
Own damage / zero-dep add-on — ₹500 extra
Personal accident cover — ₹1 Lakh (PA)
Engine protection (especially new bikes)
NCB protection (no-claim bonus retained)
Cashless garage network (near you)
Online claim process — 30-min approval
Pillion cover (if carrying passengers)
Theft + fire + natural calamity cover
Voluntary deductible = extra 5-15% discount
💡 SAVINGS TIP: 5 saal claim-free = 50% discount on renewal.
₹800 × 5 years = ₹4,000 saved (NCB retained + bonus)

THE PROTECTOR Life Insurance Checklist

👨‍👩‍👧 Life Insurance

Term plan (NOT endowment / money-back)
Cover = 10× annual income (minimum)
₹1 Cr cover with ₹500/month premium
Claim settlement ratio > 97% (IRDAI)
Premium ≤ 8% of monthly income
Tax save u/s 80C — ₹1.5L + 80C(10) ₹50k
30-year policy lock (long-term safety)
Critical illness rider (36 diseases cover)
Accidental death benefit (double cover)
Premium waiver on disability
💡 SAVINGS TIP: LIC se 2x sasta — Max Life / HDFC Life / ICICI Pru
Same ₹1 Cr cover, half the premium for non-smokers under 35
