## ⚡ Overview

A turnkey sales enablement, CRM lead tracking, and omnichannel digital marketing framework built for service professionals and financial consultants.

Features a responsive conversion-optimized landing page, CSV and Notion-backed pipeline management, automated WhatsApp lead follow-up scripts, social media marketing templates, and downloadable lead magnet PDF deliverables.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **High-Converting Landing Page:** Fully responsive mobile-first landing portal with clear call-to-actions (`landing-page/index.html`).
- 📊 **Lead Tracker Engine:** CSV and Notion-integrated pipeline with renewal reminders and daily activity logs (`lead-tracker/`).
- 💬 **WhatsApp Automation Scripts:** Multi-stage WhatsApp auto-reply and broadcast campaign playbooks (`templates/whatsapp-scripts.md`).
- 🎨 **Marketing & Social Engine:** 7-day multi-channel content calendar and high-resolution marketing poster templates (`marketing/`).
- 📄 **Lead Magnet Assets:** Pre-built, professional 5-page insurance & safety guide PDF (`assets/insurance-checklist.pdf`).
